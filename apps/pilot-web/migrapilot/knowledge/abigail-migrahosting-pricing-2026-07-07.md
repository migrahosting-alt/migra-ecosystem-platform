## Answering Guidance

- If a user asks "What are your hosting prices?", summarize Student, Starter, Premium, and Business CloudPod first.
- If a user asks for the cheapest paid hosting plan, answer Starter CloudPod at $4.99/month, or $1.29/month on the 3-year term billed as $46.44 today.

- Do not answer with older prices such as Starter $34.83, Premium $53.73, or Business $80.73 billed triennially. Those figures are outdated.
- If a customer needs custom bundles, enterprise packaging, or unclear WordPress pricing, offer a live agent handoff.
