SMS Messaging Terms & Consent

This page describes how MigraTeck LLC (“MigraVoice”) uses SMS/text messaging, how you opt in, and how to manage your preferences.

1. SMS Opt-In Consent

MigraVoice sends SMS messages only to users who have explicitly opted in through one of the following methods:

How You Opt In to MigraVoice SMS

2. Types of Messages

MigraVoice sends the following categories of SMS messages:

  • Customer Support: Two-way conversations for account support and service inquiries
  • Account Notifications: Password resets, security alerts, and account status updates
  • Service Alerts: System status notifications and maintenance updates
  • Appointment Reminders: Scheduled call-back and meeting reminders

3. Message Frequency & Rates

Message frequency varies. You may receive up to 10 messages per month depending on your service interactions. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan. MigraTeck LLC does not charge for SMS messages sent to you.

4. Opt-Out & Help Keywords

You can manage your SMS preferences at any time using these keywords:
